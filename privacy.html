Mrs Doubtfire Gmail: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Mrs Doubtfire Gmail is a private connector run by its owner on their own computer. It lets that person's AI assistant search and read their own Gmail so it can help with family and household logistics such as school deadlines, appointments and invitations. It is not offered as a service to the public.

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app requests the read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly). It can search and read messages and attachments in the account you sign in with. It cannot send, delete or change mail.

Where your data goes

  • The app runs entirely on your own computer. There is no server operated by CR8-OS, and no copy of your mail is sent to or kept by CR8-OS.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your computer, in your user folder.
  • Message content is passed only to the AI assistant you run the connector with, when you ask it to look something up.
  • Attachments are saved only if you ask, and only to a folder on your computer that you choose.

Sharing and selling

No data from your Gmail is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models by CR8-OS. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the token file from your computer.
